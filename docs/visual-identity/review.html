01 / material language

Warm invitation. Cold accounting.

Worn green baize, burgundy elbow rails, carved walnut and aged brass establish the casino through useful objects. Card shoes sit at the dealer's hand. Chip racks serve bets. Light isolates the game from the gaming floor beyond. Clinical unease enters through calibrated instruments, registration cuts and the same employee's increasingly precise attention.

One source pixel = 0.25 layout units. Curves are rasterised directly at the finer density, with native-pixel seam, engraving and anatomy work. No antialiasing in source art, no painted input, no noise filters. Wear follows contact; highlights describe planes. Pale-stock augments and lacquered powers share a print language while every card tells a different story. Read the concise art bible.

02 / recurring character

Mr. Vey, employee 00.

A silver hair crescent, crooked nose, asymmetric eye sockets, sleeve garters and practised hands. His identity survives each revelation. His smile is already present when he looks up.

An open palm, a small smile, and a delayed glance. He offers a card as though this were his hundredth dealing of the evening.

Presentation progression follows existing target-ladder progress. It creates no new penalties, rules, costs or seeded random draws. Exact triggers are specified in the integration brief.

03 / composition benchmark

Take a seat. Open an account.

The table stays intimate and subdued. The healthy machine offers the opposite invitation: stepped casino signage, ruby lacquer, polished gold, bright parchment drums, jade-glass powers and a raised cherry-red SPIN cap. Wealth and target share one window; each control keeps a stable home. There is no dealer portrait or anatomical vent in the cabinet.

The multiplier is the whole machine. At x2, enamel, metal, glass, controls and the floor catch heat. At x3, the lamp banks burn white-hot, the reel lips become incandescent, exhaust escapes from fitted vents and embers stream off the working hardware. This is maximum output, with the same intact machine and readable controls. Decay is reserved for a later pass.

Watch the whole output ladder. The comparison holds reels, wealth, target and spins fixed so the material response carries the escalation.
