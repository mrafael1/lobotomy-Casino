# Revision 03 — the healthy machine is the lure

The owner clarified the machine's role: **the main dopamine source of the game**, brighter and more enticing than the surrounding interfaces. The eventual end-of-run console will decay, but this pass concentrates on a healthy machine worth wanting to play.

## The new benchmark

The machine uses a stepped casino marquee rather than a bowed, sombre body. Ruby lacquer, polished golden fluting, warm tungsten bulbs, bright graduated parchment drums, jade-glass power caps and a raised cherry-red SPIN button establish an object in good repair. Dark room shadows keep it integrated with the house while making its invitation stronger. A dedicated healthy-machine palette leaves the accepted tables, folios, records and odds art unchanged.

The anatomical crown grille, membrane, bellows and breathing sequence are removed. The machine feels alive through **anticipation and response**: a warm perimeter chase, illuminated ready controls, a coordinated lamp response to a win, a direct score pop and coins settling into a physical tray.
